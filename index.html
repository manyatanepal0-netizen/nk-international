About Us

A Legacy of Clean & Sustainable Urban Solutions

N.K. International Management Pvt. Ltd. was established in 2000 with a singular vision — to transform how cities manage their waste and urban infrastructure. Over two decades, we have grown from a small initiative to one of the region's most trusted partners in municipal management.

Our founder envisioned a company that would bridge the gap between government objectives and community well-being. Today, with a workforce exceeding 10,000 professionals, we operate across 50+ cities and continue to set benchmarks in waste management, bus terminal operations, and environmental sustainability.

Our Services

Comprehensive municipal management and environmental solutions tailored to build cleaner, more efficient cities.

Waste Management

End-to-end solid waste management — from door-to-door collection and segregation to transportation and scientific processing across urban and semi-urban areas.

Fee Collection

Systematic and transparent user fee collection mechanisms that maximize municipal revenue while maintaining public trust and operational accountability.

Bus Terminal Operations

Professional management of bus terminals and transport hubs — ensuring clean facilities, organized operations, and seamless passenger experience.

Landfill Management

Scientific landfill design, construction, and operation following environmental regulations — with leachate treatment, gas management, and post-closure monitoring.

Recycling

Advanced material recovery and waste-to-resource operations — converting recyclables into valuable raw materials and reducing landfill dependency.

Organic Manure Production

Large-scale composting of organic waste into high-quality manure and fertilizer — supporting sustainable agriculture and closing the organic waste loop.
